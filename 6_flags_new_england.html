Description

This is a full recreation of Six Flags New England in Agawam, Hampden County, Massachusetts, United States of America. This is a Six Flags park with 12 rollercoasters. This park originally opened in 1870. It was originally called Gallup's Grove from 1870-1886, Riverside Grove from 1887-1911, Riverside Amusement Park from 1912-1999, and then Six Flags New England after that. New for 2026 is Quantum Accelerator which is an Intamin launched family straddle coaster. There is absolutely no custom scenery in these servers.
